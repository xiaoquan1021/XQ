已确诊事实(无需复查):0090_0001.vtu 与 .vtp 的真实数组分布:
- vtu: PointData={GlobalNodeID}; CellData={GlobalElementID}; FieldData=空
- vtp: PointData={GlobalBoundaryPoints, GlobalNodeID}; CellData={GlobalBoundaryCells, CapID, BadTriangle, FreeEdge, BooleanRegion, ModelFaceID, Normals, ActiveCells, GlobalElementID}; FieldData=空
